## Full Description

**QREX** is a powerful, modern Real Estate CRM built for property businesses, agencies, and developers who want to manage their entire sales pipeline from one place.

**LEAD MANAGEMENT**
Capture, track, and nurture leads from multiple sources. Score leads, assign agents, log activities, reassign ownership, and convert leads into paying customers — all from your phone. Advanced search and filters help you find any lead in seconds.

**SALES PIPELINE**
Visualize your entire deal flow with a Kanban-style pipeline. Track deal stages, probability scores, and expected revenue. Monitor conversion rates and never let a hot deal slip through the cracks.

**PROPERTY CATALOG**
Maintain a complete property inventory with rich details — type, price, area, BHK, amenities, furnishing status, parking, and possession dates. Mark properties as featured and control website visibility.

**CUSTOMER & BOOKING MANAGEMENT**
Keep a full record of your customers, bookings, and agreements. Track booking payments, agreement statuses, and possession handovers. Never lose sight of your commitments.

**PAYMENT TRACKING**
Monitor receivables vs. collected amounts across all deals. Record payment modes (UPI, RTGS, NEFT, IMPS, Cheque, Bank Transfer, Cash), due dates, and transaction IDs. Stay on top of your cash flow.

**TASKS & SITE VISITS**
Schedule and manage property site visits with agents and customers. Track visit status — scheduled, completed, rescheduled, or cancelled. Manage daily tasks with priorities, deadlines, and assignees.

**DASHBOARD & ANALYTICS**
Get a bird's-eye view of your business with real-time stats: Revenue Won, Active Leads, Pending Payments, Site Visits, and more. Explore interactive charts for Revenue vs Leads, Lead Sources, Pipeline Funnel, Agent Performance, and Monthly Bookings.

**REPORTS & EXPORT**
Generate detailed reports across Revenue, Leads, Bookings, Deals, Payments, Campaigns, and Site Visits. Export to **CSV or PDF** with date-range and agent/property filters.

**MARKETING CAMPAIGNS**
Plan and track marketing campaigns with budgets, spend tracking, and lead attribution. See which channels drive the best results and manage featured property promotions.

**MEDIA CENTER**
Upload and organize property images, documents, videos, and files. Browse by folders with thumbnail previews and storage usage tracking.

**NOTIFICATIONS & SETTINGS**
Stay informed with real-time notification alerts. Configure your agency branding, notification preferences, security settings (2FA, maintenance mode), and SMTP integration.

**ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL**
Granular permission system with module-level access (view, create, edit, delete, export, approve). Super Admin, Admin, and custom roles — users only see what their role grants them.

**BUILT FOR SCALE**
- Fast, responsive Material 3 design
- Secure authentication with encrypted token storage
- Offline-tolerant session management
- Works on Android, iOS, and Desktop

Download QREX today and take control of your real estate business.
